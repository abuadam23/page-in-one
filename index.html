Privacy Policy — Page in One

Effective date: 20 November 2026

In short: Page in One works entirely on your device. Your screenshots, the pages you capture and your settings never leave your computer. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the extension does

Page in One captures screenshots of web pages you choose (full page, visible area, a region or a freeform shape), lets you annotate and blur them, and exports them as PNG, JPEG or PDF files or copies them to your clipboard. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Information stored on your device

  • Captures — the finished image, a small thumbnail, and the title and address of the captured page, stored in the extension's private browser storage (IndexedDB) so they appear in your on-device Library. Temporary scroll segments are deleted as soon as a capture is assembled.
  • Settings — your preferences (image sharpness, PDF options, file-name pattern, retention period and similar) in chrome.storage.local.
  • Capture progress — the status of a capture in progress, in chrome.storage.session, cleared when the browser closes.
  • Local counters — how many captures you have made and whether you have seen the welcome page or rating card, in chrome.storage.local.

None of this information is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. The extension does not collect, sell, transfer or share browsing history, page content, screenshots, personal information, or usage data. It makes no network requests to any server operated by us, and it contains no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or remotely hosted code. PDF files are created locally with the bundled, open-source jsPDF library.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Finished captures are kept for 30 days by default. You can choose 1, 7, 30 or 90 days, or "until I delete them", in Settings.
  • You can delete any single capture from the Library, or everything with Settings → Delete all captures.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data.

Permissions

The extension asks only for the browser permissions it needs to capture the page you choose (reading and scrolling that page, taking screenshots of the visible tab), to assemble images (an offscreen document), to save files you export (downloads), to copy images when you click Copy (clipboard), and to remember your settings and Library (storage). These permissions are used only for those purposes.

Children

The extension is a general-purpose productivity tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
